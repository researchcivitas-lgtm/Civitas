# Civitas: Project Overview & Blueprint

**Civitas** is a nonpartisan, student-led civic research project focused on homelessness. 

The goal is to build a mini think tank model at the high school level, where students learn how to study real social problems and turn them into structured, evidence-based reports—not opinions or activism.

---

## Core Idea

Civitas exists to:
- Research real civic issues using credible data.
- Gather community input (surveys and interviews when possible).
- Analyze what’s actually happening and why.
- Propose realistic, grounded policy recommendations.
- Publish work in a clear, structured report format.

---

## Output System

Every cycle (quarterly; about 2–3 months), Civitas produces:
- **1 main report** (~8–12 pages)
- **Optional shorter articles or summaries**

### Points of Reference / Inspirations
- [Brookings Institution](https://www.brookings.edu/)
- [Urban Institute](https://www.urban.org/)
- [Carnegie Endowment for International Peace](https://carnegieendowment.org/)

### Standard Report Structure
Each report follows a consistent layout:
1. Executive summary
2. Background / context
3. Data + research
4. Community perspectives
5. Analysis
6. Recommendations
7. Conclusion + sources

---

## Summer Phase (Prototype)

The first step is a solo prototype report over the summer:
- **Topic:** Homelessness in San Diego.
- **Includes:** Data, analysis, and recommendations.
- **Purpose:** Tests whether the workflow and structure actually work.
- **Outcome:** Becomes the official template for all future Civitas reports.

---

## Expansion Plan

After the prototype phase:
- Recruit a small team of 5–10 students.
- Assign clear roles (research, writing, editing, outreach).
- Run Civitas on a quarterly production cycle.
- Maintain consistent formatting and academic standards.

---

## External Outreach

Once the prototype is complete, Civitas can:
- Share the report with professors in the mid-to-late summer.
- Reach out to local organizations and stakeholders.
- Use the published work as concrete proof of seriousness and capability.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Civitas is not just a club or a blog. It is designed to become a structured student institution that consistently produces serious civic research and communicates it clearly to the public.

> **In Short:** 
> Civitas is a student-run, unincorporated civic research “mini think tank” that turns real-world problems into structured, evidence-based reports and publishes them on a consistent cycle.
